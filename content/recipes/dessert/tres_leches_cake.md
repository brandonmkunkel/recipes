## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 cup all-purpose flour (120 g)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder (6 g)
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 5 large eggs, separated
- 1 cup granulated sugar, divided (200 g)
- 1/3 cup whole milk (80 ml)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (5 ml)

### Milk Mixture

- 12 ounce can evaporated milk (354 g)
- 14 ounce can sweetened condensed milk (396 g)
- 1/4 cup whole milk (60 ml)

### Topping

- 1 pint heavy whipping cream (475 ml)
- 3 Tablespoons powdered sugar (24 g)
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract (2.5 ml)
- Ground cinnamon, for topping

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl combine flour, baking powder, and salt. Separate the eggs into two other mixing bowls.
3. Add 3/4 cup sugar to the bowl with the egg yolks and mix on high speed until yolks are pale yellow. Add 1/3 cup milk and vanilla and stir to combine. Pour the egg yolk mixture over the flour mixture and stir gently just until combined (don't over-mix).
4. Beat the egg whites on high speed with electric beaters. As they begin to whip into stiff peaks, gradually mix in the remaining 1/4 cup of sugar. Gently fold the stiffly beaten egg whites into the batter, scraping the bottom and sides of the bowl, until combined.
5. Pour batter into an un-greased 9x13 inch pan and smooth it into an even layer. Bake for 25 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Remove from the oven and allow cake to cool completely.

### Soak

1. Combine the evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, and whole milk in a small bowl.
2. Once the cake has cooled, use a fork to poke holes all over the top of the cake.
3. Slowly pour the milk mixture over the top of the cake, making sure to pour near the edges and all around. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight to allow it to soak up the milk.

### Topping

1. Whip the heavy cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla until stiff peaks form.
2. Smooth over the top of the cake and sprinkle with cinnamon.

## Tips

- Don't grease the pan — the ungreased sides help the light sponge climb as it bakes.
- Fold the egg whites in gently to keep the cake airy so it can absorb the milk.
- Chilling overnight gives the best soak.

## Modifications

- Top with sliced strawberries or other fresh fruit.
- Add flavors like matcha, ube, or chocolate, orange zest to the milk mixture or cake batter.
